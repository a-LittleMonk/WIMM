Quick start — your first analysis

This walks through one Open Field video from start to Excel. Every other experiment works the same way; only the arena and zones differ (see Experiments).

Step 1 — Open the experiment

On the start page, choose the test in Open an experiment and click Open. (New experiment → Create designs your own test — see Custom Experiment.)

Step 2 — Add your videos

Click + Add at the top of the Queue, or drag video files onto the Queue (.mp4 .avi .mov .mkv .wmv .flv). The first video opens in the preview.

Step 3 — Check the arena

A square arena is placed automatically. Drag its four corners onto the floor of your box, or open Arena (03) and choose Manual — click 4 corners to click them yourself. The center zone follows the arena automatically. → Arena, Zones

Step 4 — Set the scale

Open Scale (05). Either enter the real cage size (Auto · cage) or click Manual line → Draw line, drag a line along something of known length (for example one wall) and type its real length in cm. Without a scale, distances are in pixels. → Scale

The arena outline sits on the inner edges of the walls; the scale (13.1 px/cm) comes from the 40 × 40 cm floor size. The red shape is what the tracker detects as the mouse.

Step 5 — Check the detection

Scrub through the video with the slider under the preview. The red marker must sit on the mouse in every part of the video. If it doesn't, open Detection (06) and follow Detection.

Step 6 — Run

Click Run all (bottom left), or the ▶ on one video's row. The row shows the progress; when it says Completed the results are ready. A copy of each video's results is saved automatically to the backup folder. → Running

Step 7 — Look at the results

Open the Output tab: Summary (all numbers), Raw data (every frame), Graphs, and the settings used. Open Tracking issues under the preview to check frames where the tracking may be wrong. → Output, Tracking issues

Step 8 — Export

In the Export tab, Combined Excel puts every video of the batch in one workbook; Export to Excel in the Output tab saves the loaded video alone. → Exporting

Doing a batch? Set everything up on the ★ source video (the first one you add), then press Run all: every video follows the source's settings, except the arena, zones, scale line, crop, animal information and any setting you changed on that video yourself — those are listed before the run starts. → Batch rules
