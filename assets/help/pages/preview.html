The video preview and player

What is drawn

  • The arena outline and the zones (with their names, e.g. CENTER 50 %, PERIPHERY).
  • The detected mouse (coloured area) and the body marker (red dot).
  • The calibration line (yellow, only when the scale is set with Manual line) and a 10 cm scale bar.
  • The legend under the video lists what is shown; on the right: the scale (px/cm), the tracking model and sensitivity.

View ▾ (top right)

OptionMeaning
MarkerOff, Center, or Center + nose + tail; its size and colour.
Info tag next to markerAnimal ID, speed and position beside the marker.
Bounding ellipseAn ellipse around the detected body.
Show trailThe path behind the mouse; length in frames (0 / All frames = whole path); Fade older segments.
Smoothing of the drawn pathMoving average of the displayed trail only (does not change the numbers).

These change only the picture, never the results.

Player

  • ⏮ ◀ ▶ ▶ ⏭: first frame, one frame back, play / pause, one frame forward, last frame.
  • 0.5× 1× 1.5× 2×: playback speed.
  • Time and frame number of the shown frame.
  • The timeline: drag to jump. After a run, red marks show frames flagged in Tracking issues.
  • Tracking issues (n): opens the check of suspicious frames.
