Questions and answers

Which settings should I report in a paper?

The Video Config sheet lists all of them, with the app version. Usually: tracking model, sensitivity, smoothing, process resolution, frame skip, min dwell, move threshold, zone sizes, scale method and px/cm, and the % of corrected frames.

How do I know the tracking is right?

Check the detection rate (≥ 90 %), the tracking issues, and watch the trajectory on a few videos. For a study, compare a sample of videos with a blinded human scorer (and, if available, another tracking program).

Can I change settings after tracking?

Analysis settings (move threshold, time bin, min dwell) and zone sizes recompute the results at once. Detection and tracking settings need the video to be run again.

Do I have to set up every video?

Only its arena (and its own zones / scale line / crop where they differ). Everything else follows the ★ source video (Batch rules). Copy the same arena to several videos with Copy arena and zones.

Why don't my zone percentages add up to 100 %?

Frames where the mouse was not detected belong to no zone.

Does the app exclude animals (e.g. too few entries)?

No. Flags such as low_activity_flag are for information; you decide.

Where are my results if I forgot to export?

Every run writes a backup Excel file per video to the backup folder (Files and folders).

Can two people analyse on two computers with the same settings?

Yes: export the detection preset (Detection → Export…) and import it on the other computer; use the same app version.
