T-Maze

Goal-arm choice and spontaneous alternation between the left and right arms.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03): the 4 corners of the rectangle around the whole T (cross-bar ends and the start-arm end).
  2. Zones (04): Start arm — where it is in the video (left / right goal arms are named as seen by the mouse running up the start arm); Arm width.

  3. Scale, check detection, run.
