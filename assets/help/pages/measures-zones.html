Zone measures

The min-dwell rule

When the mouse sits on a zone border, the detected centre jumps back and forth across it. To avoid counting each jump as an entry, a visit counts only after the body (or nose) has stayed in the zone for at least Min dwell (Analysis). Shorter blips are ignored — they count neither as an entry nor as time in that zone.

Lost tracking inside a visit

When the mouse is briefly not detected while inside a zone (one dark frame, a moment just outside a narrow arm's outline) and then found again in the same zone, the visit continues — it is not split into several entries. A gap between two different zones is never filled.

Frames where the mouse was not detected belong to no zone, so the zone percentages of a session can add up to a little less than 100 %.

Time bins

With a Time bin, the Time Bins sheet repeats distance, speed, zone times and entries for each bin; the bins add up to the session totals.
