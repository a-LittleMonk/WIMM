Messages after a run

When a run (one video or a batch) ends, the app shows what needs attention. Nothing is changed automatically — you decide what to do.

"Check these videos"

PartMeaningWhat to do
These videos failed and have no resultsThe video couldn't be tracked; the reason is given (e.g. can't open the file, frame rate unknown, arena missing or flat, perspective can't be built).Fix the cause (see Troubleshooting) and run the video again.
These videos stopped decoding earlyThe video file ended or broke before its stated length — fewer than 98 % of its frames could be read. The results cover only the part read.Check the file (play it to the end); re-export it from the camera software if damaged.
Other notesFor example a backup that could not be written (file open in Excel), an unreachable backup folder, a damaged settings file.Read the note; most say exactly what to do.

"Low detection rate"

Shown when the mouse was found in less than 90 % of a video's frames. Frames without a position are missing from distances, zone times and entries.

Follow the checklist in the message; the full guide is on Detection. Then run the video again.
