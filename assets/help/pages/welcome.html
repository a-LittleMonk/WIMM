Mice Tracker (WIMM) — Help

WIMM (Where Is My Mouse?) finds the mouse in every frame of a behaviour video, then measures where it went: distance, speed, time and entries in each zone, and the test-specific scores (alternation, discrimination index, open-arm time …). It works on single videos and on whole batches, and exports everything to Excel with graphs.

Type any word in the search box on the left (Ctrl+F) — every page and every setting of the app is searchable. Press F1 anywhere in the app to open the page of the experiment you are working on.
