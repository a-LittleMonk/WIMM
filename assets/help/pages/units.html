Units and calibration

  • With a scale (Scale), distances are in cm and speeds in cm/s; without one, in px and px/s. Never mix calibrated and uncalibrated videos in one analysis.
  • The Output tab can show other units (Distance cm / mm / m, Speed cm/s … km/h, Time s / min).
  • Every export contains all units next to each other (Excel files).
  • Times are in seconds; latencies are in the original video's time.

How accurate is the scale?

The scale is exact for the plane where it was measured (the floor). A tilted camera makes the far side of the arena look smaller: without Correct perspective, distances there come out too short. A line drawn along the top of a wall instead of the floor makes all distances too short. Check: the 10 cm bar drawn on the preview must match 10 cm on the floor.

Calibration for a study

Use the same method (cage size or line) for all animals, and check the px/cm value of every video — it should be almost the same when the camera did not move.
