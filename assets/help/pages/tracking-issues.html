Tracking issues and Auto-fix

After tracking, the app looks for frames where the position is probably wrong. Click Tracking issues (n) under the preview (n = number of flagged frames). Red marks on the timeline show the same frames.

What is flagged

DetectorFlags a frame when
Speed spikeThe step to this frame is faster than Speed > (cm/s). With Auto, the limit is raised for very active animals so normal running is not flagged.
Accel spikeSpeed changes faster than Accel > (cm/s²) — a typical sign of a one-frame jump.
Outside arenaThe position is outside the arena outline.
Gap + jumpAfter a stretch where the mouse was lost, it reappears far away.

Click a detector to turn it on or off; Recompute after changing a limit.

Checking frames

  1. Click a row in Flagged frames — the frame is shown, with the detected area / mask switches above it. The mini map shows the whole path with the flagged frames in red.
  2. Decide: is the red dot on the mouse?
  3. Yes → it was a fast real movement: Mark false-positive (it is then never changed). Unmark undoes this.
  4. No, the detected area is right but the dot is elsewhere → a tracking glitch: fix it (below).
  5. No, the detected area itself is wrong → the detection settings need work (Detection); fixing frame by frame won't help.

Fixing

ButtonDoes
Auto-fix & nextFor this flagged frame, tries replacing 1 to Max gap frames around it with a straight line between the good frames, keeps the smallest change that removes the problem without creating a new one, and moves to the next.
Auto-fix allThe same for every active flagged frame (you are asked first).
Fix positionClick on the mouse in the frame to set the position by hand.
Remove pointMarks the shown frame as not detected.
Interpolate gapsFills missed / removed frames from the good frames around them (up to Max gap).
Undo fix here / Clear all fixesUndo one fix / all fixes.
Smooth flaggedSmooths the path through the flagged frames.
Export CSVSaves the list of flagged frames.
Interpolated ◀ Go to ▶Jump between frames that were filled or fixed.

After any fix, the results are recomputed. The Summary reports Interpolated Frames, Hand-fixed Frames, Removed Frames and Corrected Frames (%), so the amount of correction is always visible in the exports.

Corrections change the data. Report the % of corrected frames in your methods, and prefer better detection settings over many manual fixes.
