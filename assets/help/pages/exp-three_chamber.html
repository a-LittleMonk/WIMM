Three-Chamber Social Test

Sociability (stranger mouse vs empty cup) and social novelty (novel vs familiar mouse).

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03): click the 4 corners of the whole three-chamber box.
  2. Zones (04):
    • Phase: Sociability or Social novelty.
    • Stranger / novel in: the chamber with the stranger (or novel) mouse — stays per video in Run all, for counterbalancing.
    • Center chamber: its width as % of the box length.
    • Cups placed: where the cups stand in the side chambers.
    • Sniff zone: distance around each cup that counts as sniffing (nose; 2–3 cm usual).

  3. Scale (cage size or line), check detection, run.
