Which graphs

GraphShows
TrajectoryThe path, with start (green) and end (red) and the zones.
HeatmapWhere the mouse spent its time (2 cm bins with a scale).
SpeedSpeed over the session.
Speed HistogramDistribution of speeds (1 cm/s bins).

TripletsY-Maze arm-entry triplets.

For each graph: SVG, PDF, PNG save it to a file; Export background White or Transparent; Preset chooses a saved graph style; Export preview shows exactly how the exported image will look.

Customize graphs (Ctrl+G)

  1. Pick the experiment tab at the top and a graph on the left.
  2. Click any part of the graph (title, axes, legend, colour bar, lines) — its settings appear on the right (text, font, size, bold, colour …). Drag titles, legend or colour bar to move them; double-click text to edit it.
  3. Apply to all graphs copies a format to every graph; Reset graph / Reset position undo changes; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo.
  4. Save as new stores the style as a preset; Update preset overwrites it.
  5. Show my data previews with your own results instead of the sample.

The preset chosen under Graph style in the Export tab is used for every exported graph. F11 makes the editor full screen.

For papers, export SVG or PDF: they stay sharp at any size and can be edited in Inkscape or Illustrator. Tick Also save SVG / PDF in the Export tab to get them for every video at once.
