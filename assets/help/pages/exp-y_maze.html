Y-Maze

Spontaneous alternation (working memory): a mouse that remembers where it has been tends to enter the three arms in turn.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03) → Y template: a Y appears. Drag the hub (centre) and the three arm tips onto your maze. Manual lets you draw arms A, B, C and the neutral zone yourself.
  2. Zones (04): Arm width (% of the shorter video side) so each arm covers the runway; Neutral zone = the hub circle (0 = off).

  3. Scale (05): Arm length = real hub-to-tip length in cm (automatic scale), or a line.
  4. Analysis (08): Min dwell — 0.5 s is common for arm entries.
  5. Check detection and run.

How entries are counted

An arm entry counts when the confirmed position changes to an arm (after the min dwell). A confirmed visit to the neutral zone ends the arm visit, so A → hub → A is two entries of A. Short blips, time just outside the drawn arm, and lost tracking inside a visit don't end it.
