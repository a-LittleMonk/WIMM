What a batch shares and what stays per video

For results that can be compared, every animal of a batch should be analysed the same way. One video in the Queue is the source, marked with a filled ★ (the first video you add; click ★ on another row to change it). Every other video follows the source's settings, except the geometry that differs per recording.

Changing a setting on one video. If you change a shared setting (for example the sensitivity) while another video is shown, that video keeps its own value; it no longer follows the source for that setting. Later changes on the source still reach all the other settings of that video. Apply to from the source makes the chosen settings follow the source again.

Before anything runs, Run all shows the source and lists every video that uses its own settings, as source value → this video's value. Press Run all to continue or Cancel to change them.

Taken from the ★ source (unless changed on a video)Kept per video
  • Detection: sensitivity, white-mouse switch, brightness, contrast, blur, gamma, sharpen, CLAHE, max blob
  • Tracking: model, process resolution, frame skip, smoothing, nose detection
  • Analysis: move threshold, time bin, min dwell
  • FPS override and trim (start and length)
  • Zone sizes: center %, cage shape and size, arm width …
  • Arena outline and perspective correction
  • Zones drawn on the video (maze arms, hub, object places)
  • Calibration line and its length
  • Crop
  • Animal information (ID, group, sex …)
  • Per-animal assignments: drug-paired side, stranger side, object roles, platform quadrant, trial type, baited arms (counterbalancing)
To check: the Video Config sheet of each export lists every setting that video was run with, plus the app version.
To try different settings on the same video and pick the best, use Multi-Run, not separate batches.
