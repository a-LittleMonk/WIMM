Manual corrections (Ctrl+K)

The Corrections button in the top bar or Ctrl+K opens a small panel for working through the video frame by frame:

ButtonDoes
Fix PositionThen click on the mouse in the preview: this frame's position is set there.
Clear all fixesRemoves every hand fix of this video.
Next missJumps to the next frame where the mouse was not found (also N).
InterpolateFills missed frames by a straight line between the good frames around them.
CloseCloses the panel.

Use the arrow keys to move between frames (Player). Fixed frames are kept exactly — smoothing never moves them. For checking a whole video efficiently, use Tracking issues first: it shows only the frames that are probably wrong.
