Trim and crop a video

Click ✂ on a video's Queue row.

Trim — which part of the video is analysed

  1. Drag the left handle of the slider to the start (e.g. the moment the mouse is released) and the right handle to the end. The picture shows the frame at the handle you move.
  2. Fine-tune with Start −1s / Start +1s and End −1s / End +1s.
  3. Check Duration in the middle (e.g. exactly 300 s for a 5-minute test).
  4. Click Save.

Latencies are reported in the original video's time (the trim start is added back), so they can be checked against the video file.

Crop — which region the tracker sees

Drag the yellow corner handles to cut away parts of the picture outside the arena (other boxes, the experimenter). Arena, zones and scale are then drawn on the cropped picture.

In Run all, the trim is a shared setting: the trim of the video shown in the panel (the settings video) is used for every video of the batch, so all sessions have the same start and length. The crop stays per video. If your videos need different start times, run them one by one with ▶, or put them in separate batches. → Batch rules
