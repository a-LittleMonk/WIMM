03 Arena

The arena is the outline of the floor the mouse moves on. Only movement inside it is tracked, and the zones are built from it. Each video has its own arena.

Ways to define it

Open the drop-down at the top of the card:

OptionWhat happens
Premade squareA centred square (80 % of the shorter video side). New videos get it automatically. Drag its corners onto the real corners.
Manual — click 4 cornersClick the four floor corners on the preview, in any order.
Auto — from contrast (now)Finds the floor from its brightness. Check and adjust the result; Auto-arena padding enlarges it slightly, because mice rarely touch the wall.
Auto — from movement (next run)Only when turned on in Extra features (Open Field): the arena is built from where the mouse went during the run.

Redraw starts again; ✕ clears the arena.

Maze tests use their own shape instead of four corners — a Y, a plus, a ring … see each experiment page.

Step by step (Open Field)

  1. Show a frame where the corners are clearly visible (scrub with the slider).
  2. Choose Manual — click 4 corners and click the four corners of the floor (the inside of the walls, at floor level).
  3. Pick Cage shape: Rectangle, Square or Custom.
  4. Check: the outline should follow the floor edges in the whole video.

Cage shape and perspective

  • Cage shape tells the app the real shape of the floor. It is used to straighten a tilted view, to draw graphs, and for the automatic scale (Scale). Custom = no automatic scale; draw a calibration line.
  • Correct perspective: when the camera looks at the arena at an angle, the floor appears as a trapezoid. Turning this on straightens the video so the floor becomes a true rectangle — distances on the far side are then correct. The arena must be drawn first. Everything (zones, graphs, numbers) is then measured in the straightened picture.
If perspective correction is on but can't be built (corners missing or invalid), the run stops with a message instead of tracking the tilted video. Redraw the corners or turn the correction off.
An arena with almost no area (four clicks on one spot, or on one line) is refused: "The arena has almost no area … Redraw it."
