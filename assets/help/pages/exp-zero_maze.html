Elevated Zero Maze

Anxiety-like behaviour on a ring with two open and two closed sections.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03): the 4 corners of the square around the ring's outer edge.
  2. Zones (04): Ring width (% of the radius); Rotation — turn the four sections until their borders match the real open / closed borders (0° = sections centred top, right, bottom, left); mark each section open or closed.

  3. Scale, check detection, run.
