What is inside the Excel files

SheetContents

Time BinsPer-bin measures (only with a Time bin).

Combined file

All Animals: one row per animal, its information first (ID, group, sex …), then every measure — paste straight into SPSS, Prism or R. Then one sheet per animal.

Every unit, side by side

Each distance, speed and time is written in all scales next to each other, so you never convert by hand:

  • Distance: cm, mm, m
  • Speed: cm/s, mm/s, m/s, m/min, km/h
  • Time: s, min

Without a scale, distances and speeds are in px and px/s and are not converted.

Raw Data columns

ColumnMeaning
Frame, Time (s)Frame number and its real time in the video.
X (px), Y (px)Body-centre position; empty when the mouse was not found.
Step Distance, SpeedDistance from the previous detected frame, and speed (distance ÷ real time between the two frames).
ZoneThe zone the body is in at that frame (where it is, before the min-dwell rule; object tests: the object the nose is at).
Arm (Confirmed)Y-Maze: the arm after the min-dwell rule — the one used for entries and times.
Nose / Tail X Y, Nose ConfidenceNose and tail positions (empty when nose detection is off).

Measure names in the Summary of the maze and object tests are short codes such as time_arm_a_s or discrimination_index: the part after the last underscore is the unit (_s seconds, _cm, _pct %). Each experiment's page explains its measures.

The same tables are also written as CSV files next to a single-video export.
