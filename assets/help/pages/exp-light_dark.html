Light–Dark Box

Anxiety-like behaviour: time in the lit compartment, transitions between sides.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03): the 4 corners of the whole box (light + dark).
  2. Zones (04): Dark side size as % of the box length (standard one third); Count lost frames as dark — when the camera can't see inside the dark side, frames where the mouse isn't found count as dark time.

  3. Scale, check detection on the light side, run.
