08 Analysis

These settings change how the measures are computed from the tracked path. Changing them recomputes the results at once — the video is not tracked again.

ControlMeaning
Move thresholdSpeed (cm/s, or px/s without a scale) at or below which the mouse counts as immobile. Gives Mobility / Immobility time, and pauses: a pause is at least 1 s in a row at or below the threshold. Off = no mobility measures.
Time binSplits the session into bins (e.g. 60 s or 300 s) and adds a Time Bins sheet: distance, speed and zone times per bin. Off = no bins.
Min dwellHow long the mouse must stay in a zone, cell or arm before it counts as an entry — and before that time counts toward the zone. Default 0.2 s; mazes often use 0.5 s.
Typical values: Move threshold 2 cm/s; Time bin 60 s (habituation) or 300 s; Min dwell 0.2–0.5 s. State them in your methods section — they are also written into every export's Video Config sheet.

Details of how each measure is computed: Locomotion, Zones.
