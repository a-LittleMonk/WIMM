Copy arena and zones to other videos

Detection and analysis settings never need copying: every video follows the ★ source automatically (Batch rules). What stays per video — the things you draw and the animal information — can be copied with Copy arena and zones… at the top of the Configuration panel.

  1. Click the video to copy from in the Queue.
  2. Click Copy arena and zones… and tick what to copy:
    • Arena outline and perspective
    • Maze arms, hub and object places
    • Calibration line (scale)
    • Exclusion mask
    • Animal information (Group, Sex, Date, Trial, Notes) — each video keeps its own Animal ID
    • Per-animal assignments (paired side, object roles, platform quadrant …)
  3. Choose the videos to copy to: all other videos (default), only videos without an arena yet, or pick them from the list.
  4. Click Copy. If a video already has some of these drawn, you are asked first.
Same camera position for several videos? Draw the arena once and copy it with the calibration line to the others — then check each video quickly, because the box may have moved a little.

Reset to ★

If you changed a setting (for example the sensitivity) on a video that is not the source, that video keeps its own value. Reset to ★ undoes those changes on the video shown, so it follows the ★ source again. Its arena, zones and animal information are not touched.

Copy from the video above or below

Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓ copy the detection settings of the previous / next video in the Queue onto the video shown. They become that video's own settings and are listed before Run all.
