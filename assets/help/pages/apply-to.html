Copy settings between videos (Apply to…)

Click the source video in the Queue, then Apply to… at the top of the Configuration panel.

  1. Fields: tick what to copy — Detection, Visualization, Smoothing/timing, Trim, FPS override, Calibration, Zone mode/cage shape/perspective, Arena/center polygons, Exclusion mask, Metadata. Select all / Clear.
  2. Targets: all other videos, only videos without saved settings, or pick specific ones from the list.
  3. Click Apply.
Same camera position for several videos? Draw the arena once and copy Arena/center polygons and Calibration to the others — then check each video quickly, because the box may have moved a little.

Copy from the video above or below

Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓ copy the detection settings of the previous / next video in the Queue onto the video shown. Its arena, calibration and animal information are not touched.

Settings copied from the ★ source make the target videos follow the source again for those settings. Settings copied from any other video become the targets' own values, and the Run all check lists them (Batch rules).
