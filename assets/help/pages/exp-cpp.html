Conditioned Place Preference

Reward or aversion: time in the drug- (stimulus-) paired compartment compared with the unpaired one.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03): the 4 corners of the whole apparatus (all compartments).
  2. Zones (04): Compartments two, or two with a neutral middle; Neutral width (% of the length); Layout side by side or stacked; Drug-paired: A (left / top) or B — stays per video in Run all (counterbalancing).

  3. Scale, check detection, run.
