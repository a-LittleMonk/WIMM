Morris Water Maze

Spatial learning (training: finding the hidden platform) and memory (probe: platform removed).

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03): the 4 corners of the square around the pool edge.
  2. Zones (04): Platform quadrant (NE, NW, SW, SE) and Platform position (distance from the centre, % of the radius); Wall zone width; Trial type Training or Probe. Quadrant and trial type stay per video in Run all.

  3. Scale (pool diameter), check detection on the water, run.
