Locomotion measures

Every experiment reports these for the whole (trimmed) session.

MeasureHow it is computed
Duration (s)Real length of the session: from the first to the last frame's time stamp, plus one frame. A gap in the recording (dropped frames) is included.
Total Frames, Frames Detected, Detection Rate (%)Frames analysed; frames where the mouse was found; their ratio. Aim for ≥ 90 %.
Total DistanceSum of the straight steps between consecutive detected positions. A missed frame is bridged: the step goes from the last detected position to the next one.
Average SpeedTotal distance ÷ duration.
Max / Median / P90 / P99 SpeedFrom the speed of every step (step distance ÷ real time between the two frames). P90 = 90 % of steps are slower. Max is sensitive to tracking glitches — prefer P99 or median.
TortuosityPath length ÷ straight-line distance from start to end (≥ 1; higher = more winding). "n/a" when start and end are the same point.
Mobility / Immobility Time (s)Time with speed above / at or below the Move threshold (Analysis). Undetected frames count as neither.
Pause Count, Total / Mean / Longest PausePauses: at least 1 s in a row at or below the Move threshold. A tracking gap ends a pause.
Interpolated / Hand-fixed / Removed Frames, Corrected Frames (%)How much of the path is not the tracker's own output (Tracking issues, corrections).

Smoothing and frame skip

Positions are first cleaned by the Smoothing filter (Tracking); skipped frames (Frame skip) are filled by straight lines. Both change distance a little, so keep them the same for every animal.

Units

cm with a scale, px without. The exports add every other unit (Units).
