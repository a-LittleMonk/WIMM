05 Scale

 

Left: Auto · cage — the 40 × 40 cm floor gives 13.11 px/cm. Right: Manual line — a 40 cm line along the bottom wall gives 13.12 px/cm. Both ways agree when the arena outline sits on the floor edges.

The scale converts pixels to centimetres. Without it, every distance and speed is in pixels and can't be compared between cameras or set-ups.

Three choices

ChoiceHowBest for
Auto · cageEnter the real inner size of the floor (cm). The scale = arena size in pixels ÷ real size. Maze tests use Arm length instead (hub to arm tip).When the arena outline is exactly on the floor edges.
Manual lineClick Draw line, drag a line along something of known length on the floor (a wall, a ruler), type its real length in cm.The most direct; use the longest straight edge you can see.
None (px)No scale; results in pixels.Only when cm don't matter.

The line below the buttons confirms the result, e.g. Calibrated (line): 13.12 px/cm. Clear removes the line.

Step by step — manual line

  1. Select Manual line, click Draw line.
  2. On the preview, press on one end of a known edge and drag to the other end.
  3. Type the real length in the cm box (e.g. 40.00 cm).
  4. Check the px/cm value; the small "10 cm" bar on the preview should look 10 cm long.

The yellow line is the calibration line: drawn along the inner edge of the bottom wall, whose real length is 40 cm.

A real length of 0 is refused: the card shows "Enter the real length of the line in cm — 0 is not a length" and runs are refused until you enter it.
Draw the line at floor level. A line drawn along the top of a wall is closer to the camera and looks longer, so distances on the floor come out too short.
With Correct perspective on, draw the line on the straightened picture. The scale is then valid everywhere on the floor.
