Radial Arm Maze

Spatial working and reference memory in an 8-arm maze.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03): the 4 corners of the square around the arm tips.
  2. Zones (04): Arm width (% of the tip-to-tip diameter); Centre size; Rotation — arm 1 starts at the top, arms are numbered clockwise; Baited arms (optional) to count reference-memory errors — stays per video in Run all.

  3. Scale, check detection, run.
