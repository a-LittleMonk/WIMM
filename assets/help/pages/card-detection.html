06 Detection

Detection decides which pixels are "mouse". The app compares every frame with a picture of the empty arena; pixels that differ enough are the mouse. These settings are the first thing to adjust when the red marker is not on the mouse.

Controls

ControlWhat it does
SensitivityHow different a pixel must be from the empty arena to count. Toward more sensitive (lower number): finds a faint mouse, but also shadows. Toward ignores noise (higher): ignores shadows, may lose a faint mouse. Default 25.
White mouse on white floorFor little contrast between mouse and floor (white on white, black on black). Makes detection much more sensitive to small differences.
Preset ▾ / ResetSaved sets of detection settings. Save stores the current settings under a name, Delete removes one, Import… / Export… move presets between computers. Reset returns to the selected preset (the built-in defaults for Default).
Brightness / Contrast / GammaAdjust the picture before detection so the mouse stands out.
BlurSmooths video noise and fur texture.
SharpenHelps a soft or out-of-focus video.
CLAHELocal contrast boost; helps with uneven lighting. Off = 0.
Max blobShapes larger than this % of the frame are rejected (big shadows, a hand). Off = no user limit (a shape larger than 20 % of the arena is still rejected).
Detected area DColours what the detector finds.
Show mask MShows the black-and-white detection picture.
View effects EShows the video with brightness / contrast / … applied.
Detection overlayColour of the detected area.

Step by step — tuning detection

  1. Turn on Detected area and Show mask.
  2. Scrub to a frame where the mouse is hard to see (dark corner, near a wall, rearing).
  3. Goal: the mouse is one solid white shape and nothing else is white.
  4. Mouse missing or in pieces → move Sensitivity toward more sensitive; for low contrast turn on White mouse on white floor; raise Contrast or Gamma.
  5. Shadows, bedding or reflections white too → move Sensitivity toward ignores noise; add a little Blur; set Max blob.
  6. Check several frames from the beginning, middle and end.
  7. Click Save to keep the settings as a preset for this camera.

After a run with less than 90 % of frames detected, the app shows this checklist. It never changes settings by itself.

To compare several settings on the same video, use Multi-Run — it tracks the video once per setting and puts the results side by side.
