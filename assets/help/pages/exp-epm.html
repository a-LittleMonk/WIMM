Elevated Plus Maze

Anxiety-like behaviour: time and entries in the open arms compared with the closed arms.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Arena (03) → Plus template: drag the centre and the four arm tips onto the maze. Manual: draw arms N, S, E, W and the center yourself. Adjust… reopens the perspective tool for a tilted camera.
  2. Zones (04): Arm width; Arm labels — mark each arm Open or Closed (this decides the open / closed totals).

  3. Scale (05): Arm length (centre to tip, cm) or a line.
  4. Analysis (08): Min dwell (e.g. 0.5 s).
  5. Check detection — open arms are often bright and closed arms dark — then run.
