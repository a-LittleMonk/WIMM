Open Field

Locomotion and anxiety-like behaviour: how much the mouse moves and how much it avoids the open center of the arena.

Set-up, step by step

  1. Add the videos; the first one opens with a premade square arena.
  2. Arena (03): drag the corners onto the floor corners (or Manual — click 4 corners). Cage shape Rectangle / Square. Tilted camera → Correct perspective. → Arena
  3. Zones (04): Center + periphery or Grid 4×4; set the center size (commonly 50 %). → Zones
  4. Scale (05): cage size or a calibration line. → Scale
  5. Check detection, then Run all.
  6. Optional: Analysis (08) Move threshold (e.g. 2 cm/s) for mobility and pauses; Time bin (e.g. 60 s) for habituation.
