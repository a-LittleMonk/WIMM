04 Zones

Zones are the areas whose time, entries and latency are measured. In most tests they are built from the arena automatically — you only set their size. Each experiment has its own zones; see its page: Open Field · Y-Maze · Novel Object · Elevated Plus · Object Location · Three Chamber · Place Preference · Light–Dark · Zero Maze · T-Maze · Radial Arm Maze · Water Maze · Custom.

Which point of the mouse counts

Zones are judged by the body centre (the middle of the detected shape). Object tests (Novel Object, Object Location, the sniff zones of the Three Chamber) use the nose, which needs Nose detection in Tracking.

Entering and leaving a zone

A visit counts only after the mouse has stayed in the zone for the Min dwell time (Analysis), so the outline flickering at the border doesn't create false entries. Short tracking losses inside a visit don't split it. Details: Zone measures.
